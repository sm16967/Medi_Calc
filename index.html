How to use MediCalc

Get a clinical result in four steps.

Step by step

  1. Pick a calculator on the home screen, or type in the search box. Use "← All calculators" to come back.
  2. Enter the values. Each box shows its unit and allowed range. Choose sex or activity level from the lists.
  3. Press Calculate. Green is normal, amber is borderline, red needs attention.
  4. Read the advice under the result. It says whether it is based on WHO guidance or general clinical guidance.

Which calculator should I use?

  • BMI: weight status for adults.
  • Body surface area: drug and chemotherapy dosing.
  • BMR and daily energy: calories needed per day.
  • Ideal body weight: ventilator and drug dosing.
  • Mean arterial pressure: blood flow to organs from a BP reading.
  • eGFR and creatinine clearance: kidney function.
  • Corrected calcium: calcium adjusted for low albumin.
  • Anion gap: helps find the cause of acidosis.
  • Waist-to-hip ratio: fat around the waist.
  • HbA1c: average blood sugar over 3 months.
  • Pregnancy due date: due date and weeks of pregnancy.
  • QTc: heart rhythm safety on an ECG.
  • CHA₂DS₂-VASc: stroke risk in atrial fibrillation.
  • Unit converter: kg and lb, cm and inches, lab units.
